# comprehension
The peoples of the five regions speak mutually unintelligible languages and differ in their tastes and desires. To convey their intentions and make their wishes understood, there are interpreters known as ji in the east, xiang in the south, didi in the west, and yi in the north.

五方之民，言語不通，嗜欲不同，達其志，通其欲，東方曰寄，南方曰象，西方曰狄鞮，北方曰譯。
